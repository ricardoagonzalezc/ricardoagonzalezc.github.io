01 — About

I'm a mechanical engineer focused on how systems perform once they leave the drawing board — from high-volume manufacturing lines to complex industrial installations. My work spans commissioning, maintenance, and process optimization across EV, beverage, and metal manufacturing.

I'm particularly interested in applying Lean methodologies, reliability engineering, and data analysis to improve OEE and operational efficiency. The projects below are where I experiment with tools and approaches for monitoring, analyzing, and improving processes — built to sharpen the same instincts I use on the plant floor.
